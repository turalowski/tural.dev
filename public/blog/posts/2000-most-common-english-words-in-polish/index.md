Some notes before you start:

- Polish has **no articles**, so *the* and *a / an* have no direct translation.
- Polish verbs come in pairs (imperfective / perfective), e.g. *robić / zrobić*. Where it helps, both are given.
- Many words have several translations depending on context; the most common ones are listed first.
